1. Introduction

You are being asked to participate in a research study investigating human navigation behavior using visual stimuli and keyboard responses.

This study is being conducted to understand how people process and respond to navigational cues.

2. Purpose of the Study

The purpose of this study is to investigate navigation performance, response accuracy, and decision-making processes in controlled experimental conditions.

3. Procedures

If you agree to participate, you will be asked to:

  • Complete this consent form and click "I agree"
  • Provide your subject ID, session number, and sequence ID
  • Participate in a navigation task that lasts approximately 10-30 minutes depending on the number of trials
  • Press and hold a key on your keyboard while navigating visual stimuli
  • Complete feedback and inter-trial intervals

4. Risks and Discomforts

Participation in this study involves minimal risk. You may experience:

  • Slight fatigue from keyboard pressing
  • Mild visual discomfort from staring at fixation points

If you experience any discomfort, you may discontinue participation at any time.

5. Benefits

There are no direct benefits to you from participating in this study. However, your participation may contribute to scientific understanding of navigation and decision-making processes.

6. Confidentiality

Your responses will be kept confidential:

  • Your subject ID and session information will be used to identify your data
  • Your data will be stored securely on encrypted servers
  • Data will only be accessible to authorized research personnel
  • Your identity will be kept confidential to the extent allowed by law

7. Voluntary Participation and Withdrawal

Participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You may refuse to participate or withdraw at any time without penalty. If you choose to withdraw:

  • You can stop the experiment at any point by closing the browser
  • Your data will be deleted immediately
  • No questions will be asked about why you are withdrawing
